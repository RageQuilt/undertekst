The TV pauses when a line has finished, so you can read the English and Danish. While paused, the app ignores Netflix's pause screen.
1. Phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi.
2. On the TV: Settings → General (or Connection) → Network → Network Status → IP Settings. Type the IP address below.
3. Tap "Open TV security page", then Advanced → Proceed. It's a one-time step that lets this app talk to the TV. A blank page or error text after that is fine; come back here.
4. Tap Connect and choose Allow on the TV.
1. Phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi. On the TV turn on: Settings → General → Devices → External devices → "TV On With Mobile" / "LG Connect Apps".
2. Find the IP: Settings → Network → Wi-Fi (or Wired) → Advanced settings. Type it below.
3. Tap "Open TV security page", then Advanced → Proceed (one time). Come back here.
4. Tap Connect and press Accept on the TV.

Phones can't send infrared from a web page, so a small Wi-Fi IR blaster next to the TV does it. It also listens, which is how the app scans your own remote.

2. Plug it in near the box. On your phone, join its Wi-Fi hotspot "tasmota-…", and enter your home Wi-Fi name and password.
